Capabilities

From social signal to buy decision

Every trend is detected, attributed, validated, and timed — so your buying team gets a recommendation, not raw data.

Trend Detection

Identifies emerging garment trends — silhouettes, fabrics, colours, patterns — from thousands of creator posts across the UK.

Causal Attribution

Not just correlation — identifies why a trend is growing: creator seeding, viral moments, seasonal demand, or price-driven adoption.

Adoption Forecasting

Proprietary models predict when a trend crosses from early adopters to mainstream, with a precise buy window in weeks.

Triple Validation

Every trend confirmed against three external signals: Google search interest, UK retail shelf presence, and runway origin.

Retail Monitoring

Daily product feeds from UK fashion retailers tracked for availability, pricing, new arrivals, and stock depletion.

Trend Certificate

One-page output per trend: growth rate, causal drivers, forecast, validation score, and a clear buy / monitor / avoid call.
