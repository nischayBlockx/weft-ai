What We Do

From Social Signal to Buy Decision

👁

Trend Detection

Computer vision extracts garment attributes — silhouette, fabric, colour, pattern — from thousands of influencer images and reels across the UK fashion panel.

📈

Causal Attribution

Not just correlation — Weft identifies why a trend is growing: creator seeding, viral sounds, seasonal demand, or price-driven adoption.

🎯

Adoption Forecasting

Diffusion models predict when a trend crosses from early adopters to mainstream, giving brands a precise buy window with lead-time in weeks.

✅

Triple Validation

Every trend is confirmed against three external signals: Google search interest, UK retail shelf presence, and runway origin — no single-source guessing.

🛍

Retail Price Monitoring

Daily product feeds from UK fashion retailers tracked for availability, pricing, new arrivals, and stock depletion across trending categories.

📜

Trend Certificate

One-page actionable output per trend: growth rate, causal drivers, adoption forecast, validation score, and a clear buy/monitor/avoid recommendation.
